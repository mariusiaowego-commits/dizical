# Analysis - 勋章墙数据库 dizical Badge Wall

## Content Nature
- App gamification system (badge/achievement system)
- Educational practice tracking for bamboo flute (竹笛) learning
- 28 badges across 5 categories with progressive difficulty

## Core Narrative
A young child bamboo flute learner journeys from first note to mastery, unlocking badges through daily practice. The badge wall tells the story of dedication, streaks, and milestones.

## Structure Analysis
- **Type**: Gamification / Achievement system explainer
- **Audience**: Parents and children using the dizical app
- **Key insight**: Badges form a journey from "first breath" to "legendary mastery"

## Badge Categories
| Category | Count | Theme |
|----------|-------|-------|
| 突破 (Breakthrough) | 8 | Firsts & rankings |
| 执着 (Persistence) | 4 | Streak chains |
| 晋级 (Promotion) | 2 | Long-term commitment |
| 巅峰 (Peak) | 4 | Historical bests |
| 段位 (Rank) | 10 | Grade exams 1-10 |

## Visual Storytelling Approach
- Each badge category → one "chapter" of the journey
- Rising visual intensity: small wins → streak fire → peak moments → exam glory
- Final spread: full badge wall as a celebration of the complete journey
