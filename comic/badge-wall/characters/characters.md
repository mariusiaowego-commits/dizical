## Character 1: 小笛童 (Little Flute Apprentice)

**Role**: Protagonist
**Age**: 8-10 years old

**Appearance**:
- Face shape: Round, childlike, with rosy cheeks
- Hair: Black, short, two small buns on top (中国传统儿童发型), short strands near ears
- Eyes: Large, expressive, bright brown, looking upward with wonder
- Build: Small, child proportions, slightly tilted posture showing enthusiasm
- Distinguishing features: Fair porcelain skin, gentle smile, holding a bamboo flute with red tassels

**Costume**:
- Default outfit: White polo shirt with pink collar and pink placket with white buttons (school uniform style)
- Color palette: White (#FFFFFF), pink (#FFB6C1), red tassels (#DC143C)
- Accessories: Bamboo flute with red tassels, hair buns with small hair ties

**Expression Range**:
- Neutral/Wonder: Eyes wide, slight smile, looking up at badge wall
- Happy/Proud: Big smile, standing taller, eyes bright
- Determined: Jaw set slightly, focused eyes, holding flute firmly
- Celebrating: Arms up or holding flute aloft, biggest smile

**Visual Reference Notes**:
Based on the grade badge placeholder descriptions from the database. This child character appears across all badge unlock scenes.

## Character 2: 勋章精灵 (Badge Guardian Spirit)

**Role**: Narrator / Guide character
**Appearance**:
- An anthropomorphic small mythical creature that lives within the badge wall
- Body shape: Soft rounded form, slightly translucent/glowing
- Face: Two large round eyes, small smile, tiny wings
- Color: Warm golden glow (#FFD700) with light yellow (#FFF8DC) highlights
- Size: Small enough to perch on a badge,泛起金色微光

**Costume**:
- Wears a tiny version of a bamboo flute player's outfit
- Small flute around its body like a guitar

**Expression Range**:
- Encouraging: Eyes curved happily (^ ^)
- Introducing: One wing pointing at a badge
- Celebrating: Jumping with joy, wings spread

**Visual Reference Notes**:
A mascot-like spirit that represents the gamification system. Appears as a small golden guide in narrator panels.
